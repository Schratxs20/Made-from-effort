Title field: Why Is Losing Weight Harder Than It Sounds?
Subtitle field: The math isn't the hard part. Staying consistent long enough to protect it is — because erasing a week of deficit only takes a couple of hours.
Content tags: Weight Loss Psychology, Caloric Deficit, Consistency, Client Coaching

This is why losing weight is harder than it sounds.

You can spend an entire week in a calorie deficit, then have one big meal, a few drinks, and suddenly a huge chunk of that deficit is gone.

Not because you "failed." Not because you ruined everything.

It's just that creating a deficit takes days of consistency, while eating enough calories to erase it can happen in a couple of hours.

That's why the real skill isn't dieting. It's learning how to be consistent for months and years, not just motivated for a few days.

The results you want come from what you can sustain.
