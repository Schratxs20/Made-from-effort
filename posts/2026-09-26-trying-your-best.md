"I'm trying my best."

Sometimes that's true.

And sometimes it's the most comforting lie we tell ourselves.

Because if you're honest, there's a difference between trying your best and doing what you're willing to do.

> Your best isn't what you say you want to do. Your best is what you consistently do when nobody is watching.

If you keep saying you want to change, but keep making the same choices...

If you keep saying you don't have time, but somehow find time for everything else...

If you keep saying you've tried everything, but haven't actually changed the things you know are holding you back...

Maybe you haven't tried your best.

Maybe you've tried until it became uncomfortable.

And there's a big difference.

The hardest part isn't knowing what you need to do.

It's admitting that you already know.

> Am I actually doing my best, or am I doing my best to convince myself that I am?
