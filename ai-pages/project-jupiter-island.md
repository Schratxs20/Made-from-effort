The Selected System

### Every Piece Earned *Its Place.*

The equipment was specified as a system, not a showroom collection. Each selection had to support the client’s training, preserve circulation in a 13-foot-wide room, and match the material standard of the residence.

01 · PRIMARY STRENGTH

#### Custom Eleiko System

A custom-colored rack, cable, and storage configuration consolidates the primary strength work into a precise footprint, supported by Eleiko rotating dumbbells and performance-grade accessories.

02 · POSTERIOR CHAIN

#### Watson Glute Blaster

A one-off [Watson](https://watsongym.co.uk/) Glute Blaster brings serious lower-body training capability into a furniture-grade form with custom upholstery and Bentley stitching.

03 · VERSATILITY

#### Watson Adjustable Bench

The matching adjustable bench carries the same Bentley-stitched finish, creating a coherent pair rather than introducing another unrelated commercial aesthetic.

04 · RECOVERY

#### Sauna, Cold Plunge + Outdoor Shower

A custom hybrid sauna combines traditional heat and infrared, with Wi-Fi control for both. A cold plunge supplied by [Chill Bunny Wellness](https://chillbunny.com/en-us) and delivered from Australia completes the indoor recovery program. An outdoor shower on the balcony overlooks the Intracoastal Waterway.

05 · INDOOR + OUTDOOR

#### Training Beyond the Room

The wraparound balcony is part of the programming. Farmers carries and other loaded movements extend outside without breaking the flow of a session.

06 · COACHING

#### Live Virtual Training

A conference-room-style video system lets Scott coach the client remotely with a stable, room-scale view—closer to an in-person session than a phone propped against a wall.

The Build

### A Ground-Up Home Gym on Jupiter Island, FL

This was a ground-up build within a Jupiter Island residence, with the brief to fit a complete training and recovery program into a room just 13 feet wide. The work began with movement, circulation, and equipment clearances before finishes or individual products were selected.

01

The Challenge

Fitting a full program — a custom hybrid sauna room, a bathroom, a cold plunge, a full equipment line, and cardio — into a room only 13 feet wide, without any of it feeling cramped or compromised.

The Solution

Every inch was planned around a custom-colored and configured rack, cable, and storage system built specifically for this footprint—precision-fit rather than sized down from a bigger room's layout. The recovery program includes a cold plunge supplied by [Chill Bunny Wellness](https://chillbunny.com/en-us) and delivered from Australia.

02

The Challenge

Standard gym equipment doesn't match the level of finish in a dream home build — the client needed the gym to feel like part of the house, not a room the house tolerates.

The Solution

Furniture-grade equipment throughout: a custom [Watson Gym Equipment](https://watsongym.co.uk/) Glute Blaster and matching adjustable bench, both finished with Bentley stitching and hand-built in the UK, alongside award-winning Eleiko rotating dumbbells—pieces specified to perform at a serious level while belonging in the architecture of the home.

03

The Challenge

A gym confined to four walls limits what training can actually look like, even in a dream build — the client wanted training to extend past the room itself.

The Solution

A wraparound balcony connects directly to the gym, so indoor-outdoor movements like farmers carries are part of the actual training flow, not a separate trip outside.

04

The Challenge

The client and I both needed a way to train together even when we're not in the same place.

The Solution

A one-of-a-kind conference-room-style video integration system built into the space, so we can train together virtually from any distance — the same as if I were standing next to him.

05

The Challenge

Recovery deserved the same level of design as the training space—including outdoors—without giving up privacy on a property that opens onto the Intracoastal Waterway.

The Solution

A custom hybrid sauna combines traditional heat and infrared, with Wi-Fi control for both. Outside, a balcony shower overlooks the Intracoastal Waterway, with privacy considered from the start.
