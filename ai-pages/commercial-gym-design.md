Why Performance Comes First

A commercial gym should make the way you coach easier—not force the coaching to work around the room.

Layouts often begin with how much equipment can fit. That misses the real system: how members enter, warm up, move between zones, receive coaching, recover, and share the floor at peak times.

Scott brings fifteen years of coaching and nearly a decade inside Equinox to the design process. That is not a substitute for a completed commercial portfolio, and we will not present it as one. It is the operational perspective behind the select commercial projects Performance Edge is now accepting.
