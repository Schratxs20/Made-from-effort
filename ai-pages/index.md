## A Process Built on *Precision*

01

### Discovery Call

We learn your vision, training philosophy, space, and goals. No templates — every project starts from scratch.

02

### Site Assessment

We visit, measure, and evaluate structural considerations, natural light, acoustics, and flow.

03

### Concept & Design

Mood boards, floor plans, and 3D renderings — everything visualized before a single item is ordered.

04

### Procurement

Equipment and materials sourced through our premium vendor network. Fully coordinated.

05

### Installation Coordination

We coordinate delivery and installation handoffs with the project team. Physical construction is handled by appropriately licensed project partners.

06

### Reveal

A complete walkthrough of your finished space. Every system explained. Then we hand you the keys.

Most projects complete in 8–20 weeks depending on scope.

## A Career in Coaching. *A Specialist Perspective on Design.*

I started training in 2010 and spent nearly a decade at **Equinox** — first as a top trainer, then as a **Master Instructor**. That career brings together private-client coaching, trainer education, and the practical demands of a training space.

My coaching work informs the design decisions: where a movement needs room, which equipment serves the training, and what should be within reach. I bring that perspective to the layout, equipment plan, and conversations with the wider project team.

**I take on a limited number of design projects at a time.** The service spans private residences, yachts, and select commercial, hospitality, and country-club spaces, with the scope agreed around each project.
