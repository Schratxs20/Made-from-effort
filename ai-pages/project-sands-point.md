# Sands Point *Home Gym*

A home training space refined over the years through new layouts and equipment updates. These selected photographs show different stages of the gym’s evolution and are not presented in chronological order.

LocationSands Point, NY

Project typePrivate residence

Work shownMultiple stages

An evolving training room

## Cardio, strength, and the room around them.

The gallery includes a cardio arrangement with the SkiErg starting at the left, as well as selected views of strength equipment, dumbbells, kettlebells, and earlier configurations. Because the room changed through the years, the images are grouped as examples of the project—not as a before-and-after timeline.

See the broader approach to [residential gym design on Long Island](https://www.madefromeffort.com/residential-gym-design), or [discuss a home gym project](https://www.madefromeffort.com/contact?project=residence&source=sands-point-project).

## Plan for the gym to evolve

These views show different arrangements rather than a single frozen layout. If a home gym may change as training needs change, plan for access to the equipment that will move, clear routes through the room, and storage that can be reorganized. The gallery is useful as a record of changing use, without implying a dated before-and-after result.
