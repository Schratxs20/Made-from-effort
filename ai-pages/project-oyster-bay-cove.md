# Oyster Bay Cove *Home Gym*

A residential garage gym in Oyster Bay Cove, New York, with dedicated cardio and strength equipment, organized free weights, and an open training area.

LocationOyster Bay Cove, NY

Project typeGarage gym

TrainingCardio + strength

Layout in a garage setting

## Make the most of the usable floor.

The photographs show the gym within an existing garage, where the overhead door system, windows, and mixed-use room conditions are part of the real setting. Cardio equipment and a strength rack sit alongside open floor area, while the rubber training surface defines the primary workout zone.

Views also show dumbbell and medicine-ball storage, a plyometric box, and the relationship between the training surface and the garage’s surrounding concrete floor. This is a completed home-gym example, not a luxury estate rendering.

Explore [home gym planning and equipment layout](https://www.madefromeffort.com/residential-gym-design) or [tell us about your space](https://www.madefromeffort.com/contact?project=residence&source=oyster-bay-cove-project).

## What a garage adds to the brief

A garage gym needs a plan for the overhead door, the transition from rubber flooring to concrete, and the path between cardio and strength stations. In these photographs, the treadmill, rack, free weights, and open area share one working room. A similar project should confirm clearances and storage before equipment is ordered.
