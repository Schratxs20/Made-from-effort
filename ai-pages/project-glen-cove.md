# Glen Cove *Restored Barn*

A render study for a light, airy training environment with the performance capacity of a private facility—planned to respect the character of a restored barn.

Location**Glen Cove, NY**

Type**Private residence**

Role**Gym design consultant**

The design direction

## High-performance training, kept light in the room.

These images are mid-project renders showing the intended direction for a restored barn. Equipment will be integrated into the architecture and selected to preserve the space’s open, warm, and visually quiet character—without compromising the ability to perform the highest level workout.

The wider property includes an indoor swim pool, cold tub, hot tub, traditional sauna, steam room, and art room. Performance Edge is consulting on the gym and has contributed expertise to the planning of several complementary wellness spaces, while the gym remains the focus of this project feature.

## What a render can and cannot settle

The images communicate the intended relationship between training equipment and the restored barn. At this stage, a render is a way to discuss layout, sightlines, and the character of the room with the project team. Final equipment locations, finishes, and installation details still depend on site conditions and coordination as the project develops.
