Project Overview

A Gym Built for

Canoe Canoe is a 2025 Custom Line 140 built around indoor-outdoor living and wellness at sea. Its training environment needed to deliver serious capability without permanently claiming a social deck or interrupting the yacht's considered design language.

The result is a complete onboard wellness system: bespoke furniture conceals the bench, free weights, yoga kit, and accessories, allowing the deck to move naturally from morning training to evening hospitality.

The Challenge

Performance at Sea, Without Compromising the Yacht

On Canoe Canoe, the same upper-deck footprint needed to support purposeful training in the morning and effortless hospitality by evening. The solution was developed with Paragon Studio and the vessel team as an integrated part of the yacht—not an equipment package added afterward.

01
The Problem

A full land-style equipment setup could deliver the training experience, but it permanently occupied a deck that also needed to function as a social environment.

The Fix

We integrated the equipment into bespoke couches and loungers. The bench, weights, yoga kit, and loose accessories live within custom joinery, so the deck can be ready for training and then return to hospitality without visual clutter.

02
The Problem

Portable equipment on a shared deck means crew has to set it up and break it down around every session and every guest changeover — a gym on board shouldn't mean added work for the crew.

The Fix

Dedicated flocked drawer inserts keep dumbbells, kettlebells, yoga equipment, and accessories ordered and secure. The system is either ready or concealed, reducing daily setup and protecting the crew's operating rhythm.

03
The Problem

Standard commercial gym equipment isn't built for salt air. Corrosion sets in on typical frames and upholstery faster than most people expect once equipment lives on deck full-time.

The Fix

The furniture was matched to the yacht's existing design language using Iroko joinery, fine slatted details, and 316 stainless-steel catches and runners. Steel training equipment was paired with a clear care and storage plan for marine use.

04
The Problem

Weight and placement on an upper deck affect stability and structural load in ways a home gym never has to consider — this isn't a decision a designer can make alone after equipment is already ordered.

The Fix

Weight, placement, fastening, and access were coordinated with the vessel team before final fabrication. Custom branding and finishes made the training system feel native to Canoe Canoe rather than brought aboard.
