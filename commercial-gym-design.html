Why Performance Comes First

A commercial gym should make the way you coach easier—not force the coaching to work around the room.

Layouts often begin with how much equipment can fit. That misses the real system: how members enter, warm up, move between zones, receive coaching, recover, and share the floor at peak times.

Scott brings fifteen years of coaching and nearly a decade inside Equinox to the design process. That is not a substitute for a completed commercial portfolio, and we will not present it as one. It is the operational perspective behind the select commercial projects Performance Edge is now accepting.

Common Questions

Before We
Plan the Floor.

Do you have completed commercial projects?

Not yet. Performance Edge is currently accepting select commercial and boutique-studio projects. The service draws on fifteen years of coaching, nearly a decade at Equinox, and completed design-management work in residential and marine settings.

Can you work with our architect and contractor?

Yes. Performance Edge serves as the gym-design and performance specialist within the wider project team; licensed construction remains with the appropriate partners.

When should you join?

Before the layout and equipment package are fixed. Early involvement prevents programming and operational needs from being forced into a room designed without them.
