Gym design in a historic home

Training equipment within the room that’s already there.

The photographed room uses a rubber training surface for the main exercise area, with cardio and strength equipment arranged around the perimeter. The existing radiator, windows, door openings, ceiling height, and circulation remain visible in the space and inform how the room is used.

This project is one example of residential gym planning in Lloyd Harbor. The focus here is the gym room itself and its fit within a historic summer cottage home—not a claim that the broader property or park was redesigned.
