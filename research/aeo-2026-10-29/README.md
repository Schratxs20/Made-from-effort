## Primary sources and implications

- [Google AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide): original useful material, accessible content, people-first SEO. No special AI schema or llms.txt requirement; Markdown is not a Google visibility shortcut.
- [Google helpful content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content): explicit first-hand evidence and useful answers, rather than generic summaries.
- [Google Article structured data](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article): author URL, truthful published/modified dates, and actual article images. Markup must agree with visible content.
- [Google generative-AI performance](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139): AI impression reporting overlaps Web search; do not add totals. Missing report can reflect insufficient data. Report dates use Pacific time.
- [Google documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates): FAQ rich results stopped appearing May 7, 2026; documentation removed in June. Useful Q&A remains worthwhile, but FAQ markup is not a Google rich-result promise.
- [Bing AI Performance](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/): citations, cited pages, and sampled grounding queries. Citations are not clicks or rankings.
- [Bing Topics Portfolio](https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/10/introducing-topics-portfolio-beta-in-ai-performance/): conditional topic opportunities; availability and sample limitations matter. Not visible in this account's observed report.
- [Microsoft content guidance](https://www.about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/october-2025/optimizing-your-content-for-inclusion-in-ai-search-answers): clear headings, explicit context and useful accessible content. Do not infer a required paragraph length or mechanical FAQ format.
- [OpenAI crawlers](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots), [Anthropic crawlers](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler), [Perplexity crawlers](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/resources/perplexity-crawlers): search crawlers, training crawlers and user-triggered retrieval are distinct. - [IndexNow protocol](https://www.indexnow.org/documentation), [FAQ](https://www.indexnow.org/faq): useful optional changed-URL notification after deployment, not Google submission or proof of indexing.
- [Google local ranking](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091): relevance, distance and prominence. Accurate profiles, legitimate reviews and credited collaborator pages help establish evidence; do not buy links or fabricate reviews.
- [Cloudflare redirect troubleshooting](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ssl/troubleshooting/too-many-redirects/) and [Always Use HTTPS](https://developers.cloudflare.com/ssl/edge-certificates/additional-options/always-use-https/): fix transport/redirect configuration before chasing editorial tactics.

## Durable editorial standard

Each substantive service page should identify who it serves, what Scott does, when to engage, useful starting inputs, scope boundaries and a clear inquiry route. Each article should answer its title's actual question early, develop a defensible judgment, use concrete examples, cite research beside the claim it supports, and connect to relevant work or services. Keep Scott's calm voice; do not turn every paragraph into search copy. No unsupported credentials, medical claims, hotel projects, measurements, fabricated client conversations or ranking promises. Hypothetical training examples must read as hypothetical. NSCA guidance and applicable ADA requirements need their actual scope, date and responsible professional stated correctly.

For original project evidence, capture: constraint → decision → training/operating consequence → actual photo or drawing → collaborator credits and permissions. The three priority articles remain ahead of the bank; this task does not publish unreviewed drafts or images.
