## Instagram / LinkedIn post

Good home gym design starts with the room and the way you want to use it.

Across Long Island, Performance Edge has worked on residential gyms in a range of homes and settings: garage gyms in Oyster Bay Cove and Dix Hills, a historic summer cottage in Lloyd Harbor, evolving equipment layouts in Sands Point, a completed Jericho estate gym, and a mid-project restored-barn study in Glen Cove.

Different homes call for different solutions. The goal is a gym that fits the space, supports the training, and makes sense for the people who use it.
